Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how "Deen Before Dunya Poster" ("the App") handles information.

1. Who This App Is For

This App is a personal tool built and used solely by its developer to manage and publish their own video content across their own connected social media accounts. It is not distributed to or used by other users.

2. Information We Collect

The App accesses only the developer's own connected social media account data (such as account ID and access tokens) necessary to publish video content on the developer's behalf. The App does not collect, store, or process any personal data belonging to third parties or other users.

3. How Information Is Used

Any account access tokens obtained are used exclusively to publish video content to the developer's own connected accounts. No data is sold, shared, or used for advertising purposes.

4. Data Storage

Access tokens and credentials are stored securely by the developer and are not shared with any third party.

5. Contact

For any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer via the contact information provided in the associated developer account.
